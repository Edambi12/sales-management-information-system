# Screenshots

(chapter 4 "Réalisation du système", figures 28–34). They were not regenerated or edited,
except for privacy redaction.

> **Privacy:** customer names, addresses, phone numbers and e-mail addresses visible in
> the original screenshots of the *Customers*, *Invoices & Payments* and *Returns*
> modules are covered by a grey **REDACTED** box. The data shown in the screenshots is the
> author's original test data, not the fictional dataset of `database/sample_data.sql`,
> so the figures differ from a fresh demo installation.
